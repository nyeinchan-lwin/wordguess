# WordGuess

A Wordle-style word-guessing game in plain HTML, CSS, and JS.

No framework. No build step. No install.

---

# Who it's for

Casual players and language learners who want a quick word game they can open right now — no app store, no account, no download.

Just a browser tab and a spare two minutes.

---

# What it does

Guess a hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries.

Each guess reveals per-letter feedback:

- 🟩 Right letter, right spot
- 🟨 Right letter, wrong spot
- ⬜ Not in the word

A new word is drawn each time you play.

---

# How to play

1. Type a 5-letter word using your keyboard or the on-screen keys
2. Press **Enter** to submit the guess
3. Read the color feedback and refine your next guess
4. Solve it within 6 tries to win

Both physical keyboard and on-screen keyboard work.
